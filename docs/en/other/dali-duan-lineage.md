# Dali Duan Imperial Lineage (In-Game Lore)

A summary of the Dali Kingdom's Duan imperial line as [Ye Yunshang](/en/people/characters/girl2) tells it
when explaining Duan Zhixiu's background. Dali is the kingdom where the [Point Cang Faction](/en/people/factions/dian-cang-sect) is based.
During the Qingcheng study-abroad route, in the event "Yunshang's Counsel" (雲裳商議), choosing the chat topic
"The young master of Songyan Pavilion" and then "the full version" has Yunshang spend the better part of an hour
drawing a genealogy chart and narrating from the founding emperor all the way to Duan Zhixiu's coup.
Choosing "in a word" gives only the short version, and the chart is never shown.

The in-game Dali closely follows the historical Dali Kingdom (937–1254, in today's Yunnan).
Temple names, posthumous titles, the string of forced abdications and the Gao clan's domination are all historical;
the fiction is concentrated in everything tied to Duan Zhixiu and in the link to the Point Cang Faction.
Deviations are noted in the "Historical record" column.

::: warning
Contains spoilers about Duan Zhixiu's origins on the [Ye Yunshang](/en/people/characters/girl2) (Qingcheng study-abroad) route.
:::

## The lineage as Yunshang tells it

"Branch" follows Yunshang's framing: the direct line of the founder Duan Siping is the first branch, the line of his
younger brother Duan Siliang the second, and the two branches traded the throne back and forth for over a century.

## Songyan Pavilion and Duan Zhixiu

Point Cang's scripture repository is the old "Songyun Pavilion" on the back mountain. "Songyan Pavilion", which Duan Zhixiu oversees,
was built on Xianzong's orders on the peak with the best view: it looks out over the whole Cang Mountain range, and looks down on the Point Cang Faction.
Point Cang and Dali city are so close that "without the walls and guards you could stroll into the palace", and a colony of martial masters next door was not something the throne could ignore.

Later, the Gao ministers long held down by Xianzong found an heir of the second branch — Duan Zhixiu, a seventh-generation descendant of Emperor Tianming Duan Suxing, then five years old — and raised a coup in his name. It failed.
The late emperor, devout like his father and unwilling to take life, spared the child, stripped him of the heir's title and of the succession to the Duke of Zhongguo's household, and exiled him to the Point Cang Faction — partly as a hostage to keep Point Cang in line.
This is why Yunshang calls his standing as keeper of Songyan Pavilion "both high and low".

Duan Zhixiu and this coup are fiction. Historically Duan Zhixing was succeeded by his sons Duan Zhilian (1200–1204) and Duan Zhixiang (1204–1238).

## Connections to Jin Yong

Besides history, the in-game Duan clan draws on Jin Yong's *Demi-Gods and Semi-Devils*. Only points with a verifiable source are listed.

- "Tianlong Temple" is the name that novel gives the historical Chongsheng Temple; the game follows it.
- Duan Zhixiu's art, the Withered-and-Flourishing Divine Art (枯榮神功), takes its name from the novel's 枯榮禪功, an internal art of the Duan clan created by Duan Siping and practised by Master Kurong of Tianlong Temple, keeper of the Six Meridian Divine Sword manual.
- Xianzong Duan Heyu (Duan Zhengyan) is the historical model for the novel's Duan Yu; Xuanzong Duan Zhixing is the model for Reverend Yideng in *The Legend of the Condor Heroes*.

Reading Duan Heyu's "formless sword qi" as a nod to the Six Meridian Divine Sword is natural, but nothing in the game says so, so it is left as a reading rather than a fact.
