## Notes
- Cancelling a development item refunds 90% of the forge/alchemy points and 90% of the silver invested.
- Once an item has been unlocked, you don't need to meet its level requirement again if you cancel and redevelop it. The prerequisite item still has to be right, though: e.g. the Heart Protector still needs the Tang Sect Uniform to be in development.
- Only items you have obtained can be unlocked. If you haven't obtained an item, it won't unlock automatically when you obtain it later, even if you already meet the development conditions.
- Data on this page is based on [活俠傳資料彙總](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10wN_Kfusr5ipzXVzvNewsPHmn3HNCNUtqME26t94I4o), [活俠傳事件及分歧整理](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1YZRvCuf7ar5eqHCEJoVRdD1uxhTtkKBBCuF_0O0OdNg) and [活俠傳事件分岐資料庫](https://mortal-web-database.vercel.app/).
